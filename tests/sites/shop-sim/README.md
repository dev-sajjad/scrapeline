# shop-sim

A small simulated shop whose catalogue changes from day to day, for testing and showing
record history (`data.record_versions`). Generated by `scripts/build_shop_sim.py`; edit the
script, not the pages. Source: `config/sources/shop-sim.yaml` (`SHOP_SIM_DAY` picks the day).

Each `day-<n>/` holds `index.html` (10 products) and `page-2.html` (the rest), linked by
`a[rel=next]`. SKUs (`data-sku`, the record identity) and product URLs
(`/shop-sim/products/<slug>.html`) are the same every day, so a product keeps one identity
whichever host serves the fixture; the product pages themselves are not served.

| Day | Products | Changes against the previous day |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20 | first snapshot; scented candle and seed tray out of stock |
| 2 | 19 | prices: espresso cups 14.99 → 16.49, monitor arm 64.50 → 69.00, linen tea towels 12.00 → 9.60, washi tape 7.49 → 5.99, ergonomic chair 1,249.00 → 1,099.00; stock: chef's knife and fountain pen sold out, scented candle back in stock; desk lamp removed |
| 3 | 21 | desk lamp back (as on day 1); new bird feeder at 16.00; espresso cups 16.49 → 13.99 |

Run in order, the days give 20 inserts, then 8 updates and 1 removal, then 1 restore,
1 insert and 1 update. `scripts/demo_history.sh` runs them and prints the price history.
